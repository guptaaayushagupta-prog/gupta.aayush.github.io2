Laboratory Protocols

Step-by-step protocols I prepared in the Holaska Lab for the techniques used in my research on emerin and nuclear envelope-mediated gene repression. Each protocol includes background on the method, safety and hazard notes, equipment and reagent lists, and a detailed procedure.

Cell culture

Adherent Mammalian Cell Subculture Protocol

How to maintain and passage adherent mammalian cell lines, such as the HeLa and C2C12 cells used in this research: detaching cells with trypsin, counting them and reseeding them at a set density. Includes a reference table of seeding densities and volumes for common culture vessels.

Indirect Immunofluorescence Protocol

PDF · 3 pages · 1.9 MB

How to label proteins in fixed cells with a primary antibody and a fluorophore-conjugated secondary antibody, showing where proteins are located and whether two proteins co-localize. This is the method behind the microscopy images in this research.

How to isolate a target protein from a cell lysate using an antibody bound to Protein G magnetic beads, capturing any proteins attached to it for detection by western blot. Includes handwritten bench notes for HeLa and C2C12 cells.
